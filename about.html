OUR DIRECTION

One platform, separated into clear experiences.

The public website is designed to keep sports discovery simple. Home introduces Betvora, Live focuses on matches in play, Football focuses on competitions, Sports covers other disciplines, Promotions gets its own space, and About explains the platform.

The authenticated sportsbook will come later, with login, registration, bet slip and account features kept separate from the public-facing pages.

Live first

Fast, readable match information is at the centre of the experience.

Original design

Betvora uses its own visual identity rather than copying another sportsbook.

Modular platform

Each major navigation item can grow independently as the backend is connected.

Responsible approach

Future betting features should be built with applicable licensing, age controls and responsible gambling requirements in mind.
